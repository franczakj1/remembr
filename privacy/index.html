Privacy Policy

Last updated October 22, 2026

This policy describes how Remembr, operated by Jason (sole proprietor), handles your information.

What we collect

  • Your mobile phone number
  • The messages you send and our replies
  • The household details you choose to save (for example appliance models, filter sizes, warranty dates, schedules) and any photos you send

How we use it

Only to provide the Remembr service: saving your details, answering your questions, and sending the reminders you set up. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Service providers

We use Twilio to send and receive text messages and an AI provider (xAI) to understand the messages you send. They process this data only to deliver the service.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Retention and deletion

We keep your saved details until you delete them or stop using the service. Text delete N to remove a saved item (N is the item number shown in list), or email jasonfranczak@yahoo.com to have all your data deleted. Texting STOP ends all messages.
